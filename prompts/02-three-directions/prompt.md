# 02 Three directions

Use after the brief, in the same session. Ask for three visual directions and choose one before anything is built. Skipping this step is why so many AI-built sites look the same.

Works in Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and any chat model.

```
From the brief and positioning above, propose three visual directions that
differ from each other in kind, not three shades of one idea.
For each direction give:
- a two-word name
- one sentence of mood, and what a visitor should feel in the first 3 seconds
- three colours with hex values, and what each one is for (ground, text, accent)
- a type pairing, one display face and one text face, and why they suit this brand
- how photographs and films should be lit and graded
- one thing it must never look like
Then recommend one and say what it risks. Do not build anything yet.
```

Give each direction a name so you can refer to it later ("build it in the Salt Light direction").
