# 01 The brief

Use before any design or code. Write six lines about the business, then let the AI question you. Describe the business before the look: "dark, glossy, cinematic" tells a coding agent nothing about what the site has to do.

Works in Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and any chat model.

```
Business: [what it sells, where, since when, how big it is]
Audience: [who buys, what they already know, what they distrust]
One action: [book / enquire / buy / join], and what happens after it
Proof I can show: [three facts I can verify, and where each comes from]
Words to avoid: [list, including words every competitor overuses]
Sector: [e.g. physiotherapy clinic], and two sites in it I admire or want to avoid
Constraints: [budget, deadline, languages, anything the client has approved]

Before you propose anything, ask me up to five questions, one at a time, most
important first. Stop when you could write the positioning yourself. Then write
it as one paragraph and I will correct it. Do not suggest visuals yet.
```
